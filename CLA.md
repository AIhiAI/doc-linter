# Contributor Licence Agreement

> **DRAFT - needs counsel review; do not publish until reviewed.**
> This is a placeholder outline, not legal text. Nothing here is binding and
> no contributor should be asked to sign it in this form.

## Intended shape (for counsel to confirm or replace)

The agreement is expected to work like the widely used Apache Individual
Contributor Licence Agreement:

1. The contributor keeps copyright in their contribution.
2. The contributor grants the project owner a perpetual, worldwide,
   non-exclusive, royalty-free copyright licence to use, modify, sublicense
   and distribute the contribution, including under licences other than
   Apache-2.0 (this clause is the reason a CLA is used; counsel to confirm the
   wording and whether a relicensing grant is wanted).
3. The contributor grants a matching patent licence for patents the
   contribution necessarily infringes.
4. The contributor confirms they have the right to make the contribution, and
   that if an employer has rights in it, the employer has permitted it.
5. A separate corporate form may be needed for contributions made on behalf of
   a company.

## Open questions for counsel

- Which legal entity is the licensee.
- Whether to use the Apache ICLA and CCLA texts unchanged.
- Governing law and how signatures are recorded.
- Whether the CLA Assistant gist should hold this text, and who owns that gist.

## Process (not yet set up)

The plan is to use the CLA Assistant GitHub app so a bot asks new
contributors to sign on their first pull request. As of this draft the app is
not installed and no repository setting has been changed.
